# Fixed-Beat VO And Visual Checks

For Remotion Shorts with locked beat starts, prepare each narration beat as its own trimmed clip and make measurable scene changes explicit in the timeline.

## What Happened

The Gua Itu Bernapas cut has eight fixed VO starts, three precisely timed impact words, a 46-second frame count, and a narrowing passage that must be visually measurable. Separate WAV clips make the start/end constraints auditable; a geometry overlay makes the passage width deterministic while the 3D cave remains visible behind it. The final render could not be validated because the sandbox denied Chromium's loopback connection.

## Takeaway

Use frame-aligned beat starts, measure every clip against its slot before rendering, and keep final-frame visual/loudness checks as a required gate. A successful TypeScript check or webpack bundle is not evidence that the final video meets visual or audio requirements.
